# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard)

## 📍 Project Context
This project analyses retail sales data from a fictional coffee shop chain, **Maven Roasters**, operating across three NYC store locations: Astoria, Hell’s Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan. The goal is to uncover patterns in customer behaviour, product performance, and store-level trends to help business owners make smarter decisions around staffing, inventory, and promotions.

## 📂 Dataset Background
The dataset comes from [Maven Analytics Data Playground](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/coffee-shop-sales?order=date_added%2Cdesc&search=coffe+shop), featuring transaction records including:

- **Transaction Date & Timestamp**: Enables time-based analysis (daily, hourly, monthly trends)
- **Store Location**: Allows comparison across geographic performance
- **Product-Level Details**: Includes product name, category, size, and price

### 🔍 Recommended Analysis Questions
- How have Maven Roasters sales trended over time?
- Which days of the week tend to be busiest, and why?
- What times of day are most popular? Do trends vary by location?
- Which products are sold most and least often? Which drive the most revenue?

## ❓ Problem Statement
How can a coffee shop optimise its operations and revenue by understanding sales trends, peak hours, and product preferences?

This analysis is useful for store managers, marketing teams, and business owners looking to improve customer engagement and profitability.

## 💡 Key Insight
- Morning hours (especially 7–11 AM) drive the highest transaction volume.
- Regular and large-sized coffees dominate sales, and five espresso-based products contribute over 20% of total revenue.
- Targeted promotions during peak hours and bundling top products could significantly boost revenue.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Excel Pivot Tables**: Summarised sales by time, product, and location.
- **Power Query**: Cleaned and transformed raw data efficiently.
- **Advanced Formulas**: Calculated KPIs like average spend and transaction volume.
- **Conditional Formatting & Charts**: Visualised trends and performance metrics.

## 🔄 Process Walkthrough
- Filtered data to focus on active stores and recent months.
- Defined KPIs, including total revenue, average spend, and product-level performance.
- Grouped transactions by hour, product category, and store location.
- Identified patterns in peak hours, top-selling items, and customer preferences by size.

## 🏆 Key Findings
- **Total Revenue**: $698,812.33 across all 3 stores.
- **Peak Sales Hours**: The highest transaction volume occurs in morning hours (7-11 AM).
- **Top 5 Products**: Brazilian, Ethiopia, Jamaican Coffee River, Latte, and Sustainably Grown Organic, coffees generated $194,266.60 in revenue.
- **Top 5 Product Types**: All espresso variants of the top products contributed significantly to sales.
- **Store Performance**: Astoria and Hell’s Kitchen each recorded approximately 51,000 visitors, while Lower Manhattan followed with 48,000. Revenues across these stores ranged between $230,000 and $237,000.
- **Product Size Preferences**: Large and regular-sized coffees dominate, with 44,885 and 45,789 transactions, respectively.
- **Average spend**: $4.49 per sales ; **Average Order Value**: 1.44

## 🧠 Reflections
- Improving morning-hour promotions and bundling high-performing products could increase average spend.
- Store-specific strategies may help underperforming locations catch up.
- Product categories with lower performance may benefit from seasonal campaigns or repositioning.

## ⚠️ Limitations
- No customer demographic or loyalty data available.
- Pricing and discount history not included.
