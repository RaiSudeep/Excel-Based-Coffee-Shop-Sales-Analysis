## 📍 Project Overview  
This project analyses retail sales data from a fictional coffee shop chain, **Maven Roasters**, operating across three NYC store locations: Astoria, Hell’s Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan. The goal is to uncover patterns in customer behaviour, product performance, and store-level trends to help business owners make smarter decisions around staffing, inventory, and promotions.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement  
Coffee shops operate in competitive environments where customer loyalty, product mix, and seasonal demand directly affect profitability.  

This project addresses the challenge:  
**_How can transactional sales data be used to identify top‑selling products, optimise pricing strategies, and improve customer retention?_**

## 📂 Dataset Background
The dataset comes from [Maven Analytics - Coffee Shop Sales](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/coffee-shop-sales?order=date_added%2Cdesc&search=coffe+shop), featuring transaction records including:

- **Transaction Date & Timestamp**: Enables time-based analysis (daily, hourly, monthly trends).
- **Store Location**: Allows comparison across geographic performance.
- **Product-Level Details**: Includes product name, category, size, and price.

### 🔍 Recommended Analysis Questions
- How have Maven Roasters sales trended over time?
- Which days of the week tend to be busiest, and why?
- What times of day are most popular? Do trends vary by location?
- Which products are sold most and least often? Which drive the most revenue?

## 💡 Key Insights  
- **Morning hours (7–11 AM)** drive the highest transaction volume, accounting for over 50% of daily orders.  
- **Coffee and tea** are the top revenue categories, contributing **67% of total sales**.  
- **Barista Espresso and Brewed Chai Tea** are the highest‑earning product types, generating over **$168K combined**.  
- **Top 5 products** (Brazilian, Ethiopian, Jamaican Coffee River, Latte, and Organic) contribute **~28% of total revenue**.  
- **Regular and large‑sized drinks** dominate order volume, each representing ~30% of total transactions.  
- **Hell’s Kitchen** is the best‑performing store location, slightly ahead of Astoria and Lower Manhattan in both footfall and revenue.  
- **Sales peak in May and June**, indicating seasonal demand or successful promotions.  
- **Weekday sales are consistent**, with Monday and Friday showing the highest revenue.

## 📌 Expected Outcomes & Insights  
**Q1: How have Maven Roasters sales trended over time?**  
A1: Sales show steady growth across months, with **peaks in May and June**, reflecting seasonal demand and promotional campaigns.  

**Q2: Which days of the week tend to be busiest, and why?**  
A2: **Weekdays (Monday & Friday)** consistently record the highest revenue, driven by commuter traffic and routine coffee purchases.  

**Q3: What times of day are most popular? Do trends vary by location?**  
A3: **Morning hours (7–11 AM)** dominate across all locations, with Hell’s Kitchen showing slightly higher footfall during peak hours compared to Astoria and Lower Manhattan.  

**Q4: Which products drive the most revenue, and which underperform?**  
A4: **Coffee and tea** together account for nearly **67% of total sales**, with espresso‑based drinks and chai tea leading revenue. Packaged chocolate and branded items contribute the least, highlighting opportunities to adjust inventory or marketing focus.  

**Q5: How do customers respond to product sizing?**  
A5: Regular, large, and not-defined sizes each represent ~30% of orders, showing balanced customer preferences.  

**Q6: Which store location performs best?**  
A6: **Hell’s Kitchen** records the highest footfall and revenue, slightly ahead of Astoria and Lower Manhattan.  

**Q7: What strategies could further boost revenue?**  
A7: **Targeted promotions during peak hours** and **bundling top-selling products** could significantly increase revenue.
